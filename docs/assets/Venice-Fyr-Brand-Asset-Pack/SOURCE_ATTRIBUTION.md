# Source Attribution and Brand Constraints

## Repository source

- Repository: `https://github.com/spearchucker667/Venice-Fyr`
- Product branding policy: `docs/BRANDING.md`
- Machine-readable Venice brand kit: `docs/assets/venice-brand-kit/DESIGN.md`
- Official brand guidelines PDF: `docs/assets/venice-brand-guidelines.pdf`

## Official Venice sources

- Live brand kit: `https://venice.ai/brand`
- The official crossed-keys vectors in `sources/official/` preserve the source geometry and approved ink colors from the repository's vendored Venice brand kit.
- The live Venice brand page currently provides approved wordmark, keys, lockup, and Built in Venice integrator badge downloads.

## Product artwork

`Venice Fyr Beacon v2` is a product-specific mark derived from the repository's existing custom beacon/lighthouse identity and official Venice color system. It is **not** an official Venice corporate mark and must not be represented as one.

## Fonts

No font binaries are included in this pack. The official brand guidance references Canela, Aeonik, and Aeonik Fono; their redistribution/licensing must be handled separately.
